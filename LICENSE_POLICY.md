# AIProgramming Asset License Policy

## Policy for v0.1

The centralized starter library uses only packs whose current source page declares Creative Commons CC0 1.0.

Every pack must have:
- a stable `source_page`;
- a declared license in `catalog/sources.json`;
- download provenance written during build;
- SHA-256 of the downloaded source archive.

## Why this is strict

AIProgramming may automatically reuse assets across many student projects. Assets that are allowed inside a finished game but prohibit redistribution as an asset library are not suitable for this shared-library model without separate permission.

## Do not auto-add

- random internet images or models without an explicit license;
- Google Images results;
- paid Asset Store packs;
- assets with redistribution restrictions unless separately licensed for this use;
- attribution-required assets until an attribution pipeline is implemented.
